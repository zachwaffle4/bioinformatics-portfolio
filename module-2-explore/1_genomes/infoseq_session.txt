# How many sequence files are in the worm genome?
# 7: chromosomes I, II, III, IV, V, X and the mitochondrion (NC_001328.1).

# How many sequence files are in the Borrelia genome?
# 22: 1 chromosome (NC_001318.1) and 21 plasmids.

# Which sequence in the worm genome has the lowest GC%?
# NC_001328.1, the mitochondrion, at 23.78%. The nuclear chromosomes range from 34.59% to 36.20%.

# Which plasmid in Borrelia has the highest GC%? What is interesting about this plasmid?
# lp28-1 (NC_001851.2), at 32.75%. The next highest is lp28-2 at 31.64%; the chromosome is 28.60%.
# lp28-1 carries the vls locus (including vlsE), which Borrelia uses for antigenic variation to
