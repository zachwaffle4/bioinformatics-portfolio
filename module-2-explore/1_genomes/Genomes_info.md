# Genomes_info

## Borrelia burgdorferi B31 (Lyme disease bacterium), GCF_000008685.2

- **Size:** ~1.5 Mb total; chromosome 910,724 bp, plus 21 plasmids (5,228 to 53,657 bp)
- **Structure:** 1 linear chromosome; 21 plasmids, of which 12 are linear (lp5, lp17, lp21, lp25, lp28-1 to -4,
  lp36, lp38, lp54, lp56) and 9 are circular (cp9, cp26, cp32-1, -3, -4, -6, -7, -8, -9)
- **Protein-coding genes:** 8136
- **Access:** NCBI Datasets / FTP, accession GCF_000008685.2. Local copy: `data/genomes/B_burgdorferi_genomic.fna.gz`

## Caenorhabditis elegans (lab worm), GCF_000002985.6

- **Size:** 100,286,401 bp (~100.3 Mb)
- **Structure:** 6 nuclear chromosomes (I, II, III, IV, V, X) plus the mitochondrial genome (13,794 bp); no plasmids
- **Protein-coding genes:** 19983
- **Access:** NCBI FTP, accession GCF_000002985.6. On Explorer only (`genomes/ref/C_elegans_genomic.fna`, 97 MB)

## Parastichopus parvimensis (sea cucumber)

- **Size:** 873.1 Mb
- **Structure:** 21,559 scaffolds; no chromosomes?
- **Protein-coding genes:** 48
- **Access:** course data directory, copied to `inclass/fastq_files/Sea_cuke` on Explorer

## Homo sapiens, GRCh38 (GCA_000001405.29)

- **Size:** ~3.1 Gb
- **Structure:** 22 autosomes, X, Y, and the mitochondrial genome; the primary assembly excludes alternate haplotypes
- **Protein-coding genes:** ~19000-20000
- **Access:** Ensembl release 116, `Homo_sapiens.GRCh38.dna_sm.primary_assembly.fa.gz` (soft-masked). On Explorer only
  (`genomes/ref/H_sapiens_primary_assembly.fa`, 3.0 GB). Annotation: Ensembl release 115 GTF

## Lambda phage, NC_001416.1

- **Size:** ~48.5 kb
- **Structure:** linear double-stranded DNA, single genome (the ends are sticky and it circularizes after infection)
- **Protein-coding genes:** ~40
- **Access:** NCBI nucleotide, NC_001416.1. Local copy: `data/genomes/lambda_virus.fa`
